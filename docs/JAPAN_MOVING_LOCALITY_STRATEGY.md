# JAPAN MOVING LOCALITY & JURISDICTIONAL STRATEGY

**Architecture**: Toolio Life Event & Housing Governance  
**Scope**: Municipality Differences, Inter-Prefectural Rules, Special Ward Policies & Privacy Safeguards  

---

## 1. Locality Architecture & Privacy Safeguards

### 1.1 Municipality-Level Abstraction Standard
Under Toolio's zero-risk privacy guidelines:
- **No Street Address or Room Numbers**: Calculations and checklists operate solely on the scope of relocation (Intra-city `same_municipality` vs Inter-city `different_municipality`) and postal distance brackets.
- **Client-Side Processing**: All profile inputs and checklist states are processed 100% locally in the user's browser. Zero personal identifiable information (PII) is transmitted to external servers.

## 2. Intra-Municipality vs Inter-Municipality Governance

### 2.1 Intra-Municipality (同一市区町村内の引越し)
When moving within the same city, town, village, or special ward:
1. **Single Procedure — Tenkyo-todoke (転居届)**:
   - Governed by Basic Resident Registration Act Article 23.
   - Submitted within 14 days after moving in.
   - **No Move-out Notice (転出届) is needed**; former address is updated within the existing resident register.
2. **National Health Insurance (国民健康保険) & Pension (国民年金)**:
   - Address updated simultaneously with Tenkyo-todoke. No cancellation or new qualification certificate required.
3. **Child Allowance (児童手当)**:
   - Benefit recipient certification transfers automatically within the same municipal budget. No new application (認定請求) required.
4. **Water and Sewage (上下水道)**:
   - In most municipalities (e.g. Tokyo Bureau of Waterworks), single online account transfer handles both stop and start.

### 2.2 Cross-Municipality (異なる市区町村への引越し)
When crossing municipal boundaries:
1. **Dual Statutory Procedure**:
   - **Step 1: Tenshutsu-todoke (転出届)** to former municipality $\to$ obtains Move-out Certificate (転出証明書) or electronic record.
   - **Step 2: Tennyu-todoke (転入届)** to new municipality within 14 days of move-in date.
2. **Child Allowance 15-Day Critical Rule**:
   - Former municipality terminates allowance at end of move-out month.
   - New municipality requires a brand-new Certification Application (認定請求書).
   - Under Article 8 of the Child Allowance Act, application must be submitted within **15 days of the day after move date** to guarantee continuity.
3. **National Health Insurance Re-Enrollment**:
   - Must surrender former health card and apply for new insurance card at the new municipal counter within 14 days.

## 3. Special Ward (Tokyo 23 Wards) & Ordinance-Designated Cities

### 3.1 Tokyo 23 Special Wards (東京都特別区)
- Each special ward (e.g. Shinjuku-ku, Shibuya-ku, Minato-ku) functions as an **independent municipality** under local autonomy laws.
- Moving from Shinjuku to Shibuya is treated as a **cross-municipality move** requiring full Tenshutsu and Tennyu procedures.
- Exception: Metropolitan Police Department (Keishicho) manages driver license address endorsements across all 23 wards uniformly.

### 3.2 Ordinance-Designated Cities (政令指定都市 — Osaka, Yokohama, Nagoya, etc.)
- Divided into administrative wards (Ku).
- Moving between wards in the same city (e.g., Osaka-shi Kita-ku to Chuo-ku) requires **Tenkyo-todoke (転居届)**, but municipal tax accounts and municipal health insurance pools remain within the same city authority.

## 4. Vehicle & Property Jurisdictional Boundaries

### 4.1 Land Transport Bureau Jurisdictions (運輸支局・軽自動車検査協会)
- When a move crosses the jurisdiction of the Land Transport Bureau (e.g., Shinagawa plate jurisdiction to Tama plate jurisdiction, or Tokyo to Kanagawa):
  - In addition to updating the Vehicle Inspection Certificate (車検証) within 15 days, **license plates must be physically exchanged**.
  - A Garage Certificate (車庫証明) issued by the local police station having jurisdiction over the new parking space is a prerequisite.

### 4.2 Bicycle Crime Prevention Registration (自転車防犯登録)
- Bicycle registries are operated autonomously by **Prefectural Bicycle Crime Prevention Associations**.
- If moving across prefectural lines:
  - Registration cannot be transferred.
  - Shipper must cancel former prefectural registration and re-register at a bicycle shop in the new prefecture (~600–700 JPY fee).
